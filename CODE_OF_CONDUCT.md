# Code of Conduct — maatify/php-seo

[![Maatify SEO](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-SEO-blue?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify/php-seo)
[![Maatify Ecosystem](https://img.shields.io/badge/Maatify-Ecosystem-9C27B0?style=for-the-badge)](https://github.com/Maatify)

## Scope

This policy applies to participation in the Maatify SEO repository and project spaces, including pull requests, issues, discussions, and project-related events or communications.

## Expected Conduct

Participate respectfully and constructively. Discuss ideas and work on their merits, welcome differing viewpoints, and keep feedback relevant to the project.

## Unacceptable Conduct

Harassment, discrimination, threats, hateful or abusive language, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing, intimidation, and sustained disruptive behavior are not acceptable. Do not publish another person's private information without permission.

## Maintainer Response

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this policy. They may remove or edit offending content, issue a warning, restrict participation, or suspend or block a participant from project spaces. They will consider the circumstances and may take the action they judge appropriate to protect participants and the project.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to **support@maatify.com**. Include the relevant project location, a description of what happened, and any context that can help the maintainers review it. Reports will be handled as discreetly as practical.

Security vulnerabilities must also be reported privately to that address; do not post them in public project spaces. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
